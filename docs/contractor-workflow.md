# Contractor and landscaper workflow

Documentation clarification, 12 November 2026. This is the intended Design Studio journey, mapped to the approved roadmap. A destination below describes the workflow, not a claim that every screen is fully connected. Read the canonical feature register for implementation and acceptance status. The [concept reconciliation](UI_CONCEPT_RECONCILIATION.md) preserves the differences between the HTML, catalogue and inspected repository.

## On-screen journey (15 November 2026)

Design Studio presents the ten steps above as four on-screen steps in the project map. This change is presentation and navigation only; checks, permissions and roadmap IDs are unchanged.

- **1 Site** covers steps 3–4: site records, a site summary with estimated area and provenance, facts still to check, and existing-site items.
- **2 Design** covers step 5: Layout, Trees, Objects and Materials, with one option picker on the map.
- **3 Prices** covers steps 7–8: Pricing and Saved rates.
- **4 Proposal** covers steps 8–9: Review (what needs attention, plus an Option A/B comparison), Total, and Proposal with preview, issue and retained PDF.

Steps 1–2 (project and brief) remain on the project page. The brief checkpoint and the step 10 change register sit in its "Brief agreement, changes, history and access" disclosure. Step 6 shade checks are under More tools → Shade study. See [UI design](UI_DESIGN.md#design-studio-journey-simplification--15-november-2026).

## Quantity and pricing handoff

The five stages are visual element -> stated measurement method/geometry -> compatible sourced dated rate -> preliminary estimate -> explicit partial/complete readiness. The [measurement and estimating contract](measurement-and-estimating.md) defines supported paving, path, fence and planting methods, plus the L31 limited road/driveway surface-layer takeoff. An object can remain unpriced, nonbillable context or explicitly outside the quote; none should silently become a free priced item. L31 is a partial implementation: it supports a deliberately drawn area and user-specified uniform material layers. The town-building-style drag road tool is proposed under L31 and does not yet create road geometry in the working UI. Engineered road design and full proposal disclosure of civil assumptions remain open.

All ten required workflow steps map to existing roadmap/refinement IDs. Their complete implementation still needs feature-by-feature evidence. L31 was added to the project roadmap by explicit user request on 12 November 2026; it is an addendum to, not part of, the original 84-feature PDF baseline. Full vehicle-road engineering and verified terrain/pond earthworks costing remain outside its bounded scope. Terrain/pond earthworks remains the separate proposed `PROP-EARTH-01` decision.

## Separate Organisation Review journey

An owner team selects **Organisation Review**, prepares a common brief/baseline (O02-O04), receives versioned supplier packages (O05/O06), confirms common scope and cost normalisation (O07/O13), sets the rubric and reviews comparable evidence (O08-O10/O14/O26), calculates official results under agreed rules (O12), records clarifications and authorised signoff (O15/O16), then retains/exports the decision pack (O17/O18/O28). Shared disclosure, file, history and access rules still apply.

The HTML's F14 internal option preference/decision brief is a limited planning aid. It does not implement this procurement/evaluation journey, does not publish a contractor's private costs, and does not establish sealed tender or committee consensus. Those broader O features keep their existing priorities and statuses.
