## Five distinct stages

1. **Draw or place an element.** Record stable ID, kind, existing/proposed status, option, dimensions/geometry, source and revision. Its visual presence alone does not establish a measurable or billable scope.
2. **Derive a reviewable quantity.** Declare the supported geometry, suitable projected metric CRS, method, units and relevant assumptions. Preserve measured/sourced/estimated/unknown provenance; a calculation from uncertain geometry stays uncertain. Retain the calculated value alongside any admitted active override and its evidence.
3. **Select a compatible rate.** Deliberately link the applicable immutable rate version. Record source, effective date, validity, unit, currency, region/specification and inclusions. The current reviewed currency is MYR. Rate amount, scope and basis must match the actual billable quantity or a named assembly component. A saved source is not independent supplier verification.
4. **Calculate the preliminary estimate.** Only valid priced lines contribute to the priced subtotal. Show other intended work, reasons it is unpriced/invalid, and explicit exclusions. Changes to geometry, method, recipe, rate selection, quantities or pricing require appropriate draft review/recalculation; they do not rewrite issued snapshots.
5. **State completeness and review readiness.** A subtotal with missing billable prices is partial/incomplete, never a full quotation. Technical completeness does not establish supplier confirmation, site accuracy, all-in construction cost or customer acceptance. Current source/validity/zero/optional/provisional warnings must be resolved or treated under the existing authorised issue-review policy.

## Rates, charges and failure states

- Preserve missing rate, incompatible quantity unit/currency/rate basis, invalid or stale measurement, unapproved recipe and stale result as explicit states. Valid lines may give a **priced subtotal**; invalid lines never become successful zero-cost scope. Deliberately zero rates/quantities require review.
- Source/effective date/validity and inclusions are separate fields. Unknown/expired/future validity, archive state, zero values, proposal validity beyond rate expiry, optional/provisional scope and below-listed-cost selling have existing readiness/exception rules. These are not all identical hard blockers. Missing billable rates and unit mismatch cannot be overridden into a complete quotation.
- Demo/fictional rates stay labelled in every estimate, comparison and exported example. Never describe them as supplier quotes or current market prices. Review of real source/region/specification remains a commercial gate; no rate is invented from an object's appearance.
- Record labour, equipment, transport, overhead, contingency and other scope explicitly in an inclusive rate, approved components or permitted charges. Do not add them twice. Do not invent productivity factors. Unknown scope remains visible even when a zero tax or explicit exclusion is recorded.
- Recipe material waste applies once. Current base estimator applies global waste only to eligible measured area/length lines, not counts, fixed charges or already-derived recipe components. Installation quantity and procurement quantity are distinct where the recipe declares them.
- Current pricing uses backend Decimal, quantities/rates at four decimals and line/selling amounts at two decimals with HALF_UP. Markup is cost base x (1 + percent/100); margin is cost base / (1 - percent/100). These are alternatives, not a compound surcharge. Fixed selling has its own reviewed basis. No implicit tax, discount or escalation is supplied.
- A cost amount is not automatically a customer selling price. Respect internal-cost capability and rate-basis rules. Current indicative versus cost rates are validated separately; do not silently mark up a final selling-price rate.
- Work marked existing is excluded from proposed-work pricing. Optional/provisional line status is visible and, in the current estimator, remains included in the stated total until scope is deliberately changed. Hiding a layer is display-only and never deletes billable work.

## Readiness and revision boundaries

The [pricing/proposal implementation contract](pricing-and-proposals.md) governs current saved calculation/issue behaviour. [Plant/material schedules](plant-material-schedules.md) gives the implemented recipe details. A complete estimate is complete only for its declared included scope and listed assumptions. It is still preliminary until relevant site, commercial and customer acceptance gates are satisfied.

New rate records do not silently replace selected versions. The main HTML's live numeric-rate update is a demonstration shortcut; the embedded gallery and repository use deliberate adoption/recalculation. Save a new result/issue after relevant changes; preserve earlier source manifests and original issued bytes.

Client proposal and internal Organisation Review decision report are different audiences and contracts (L24 versus O17). Field-unvalidated numerical shade must not be inserted into production proposals merely because synthetic figures appear in the concept. Private costs/profit and unselected private evidence never leak through a client presentation.
