INTERACTIONS
Map pan, zoom, reset (Studio), feature information popups; Studio Baseline/A/B
switching and layer visibility; terrain tool selection and brush-size control;
review notes/preferred option and explicit local-browser save.
Sidebar links are preview placeholders. The workspace dropdown switches views.

LIMITS
Terrain tool controls do not modify elevations. Cut/fill, quantities and costs
are labelled sample values, not calculated from drawn geometry. Proposed work
is illustrative and not a surveyed layout. No real organisation project is
read or modified. Local preview storage uses its own namespaced keys. No
backend authorisation, submission exchange or formal approval is implied.
Retain the visible map-provider attribution when reproducing the images.
