## Purpose

Place editable proposed road/driveway concept footprints and prepare a traceable preliminary takeoff from user-supplied layer specifications and rates.

## Trigger and prerequisites

Authorised saved Design Studio workspace with boundary and geometry grant; select Option A/B and Layout > Roads and driveways. Enter source/measurement assumptions and explicit width for line/curve routes. Existing area-polygon takeoff remains supported.

## Inputs and sources

WGS84 polygon or specifically supported site geometry; original/source metadata; measurement provenance and uncertainty; user-supplied road/driveway name and scope status; explicit surface and layer definitions (material/specification, thickness, quantity unit, inclusions, optional/provisional status); immutable dated compatible unit rates and source/region/currency; user-supplied waste or density/conversion factors with source and applicability where required. No default design section, compacted density, bulking factor, asphalt rate, soil class, survey surface or jurisdiction rule is inferred. Proposed NEXT UI inputs: pointer-drawn route, user-adjustable width, editable curve controls, selected option and geometry/source attribution. These are proposed interaction requirements, not existing accepted engineering methods. Implemented bounded interaction: WGS84 control points (2-12 for routes, 3-12 for areas), line/curve/area mode, explicit positive width up to 100 m, source and provenance. Curves use sampled Catmull-Rom segments in the backend UTM frame.

## Outputs

Bounded road/driveway concept drawing: drag or click a line route, smooth curve or area shape; edit up to 12 control points and explicit constant width; preview and confirm a proposed option-owned footprint. Backend derives the footprint in the site UTM CRS with flat caps/rounded joins, rejects invalid/out-of-bound or overlapping separate roads, and reuses revisioned saves, road layers, compatible sourced component rates, preliminary estimates and BOQ export. No engineered alignment, pavement design, earthworks, drainage, compaction or certification. Confirmed footprint, route metadata, quantity basis and selected layer/rate records persist through the existing workspace and immutable estimate revision paths. Missing layers/rates stay visibly unpriced; no complete-construction-scope claim. 12 November town-builder continuation: Town-builder road UI adds Dirt/Pavement/Stone/Gravel visual surface tiles, straight/bends/curve/area buttons, visible editable 4 m concept drawing-width preset and slider. Source notes default explicitly to estimated concept sketch, with detailed notes and numeric coordinates expandable. Surface metadata and procedural textures persist on map roads; appearance does not assign assembly, thickness or price. Existing footprint validation, layer quantities and pricing remain authoritative. 13 November 3D texture continuation: saved Dirt/Pavement/Stone/Gravel patterns and all six grass-paint looks now persist in geographic and local metric 3D, including scenario switches and reload. Original pixels/strokes are reused; paint keeps clipping/erasing and follows the terrain. Road triangles share rendered ground planes and update through sculpting, avoiding breaks at hills. Appearance is visual only; quantities, rates and issued report schematics remain unchanged.

## Validation and failure states

Reject invalid/out-of-bound/self-intersecting geometry, unsupported CRS/units, missing thickness/material/specification, incompatible rate units/currency/basis, unreferenced conversion factors, incomplete or stale inputs, and stale expected revisions. Do not silently repair, convert tonnes/volume, assign zero or market prices, or mark the estimate complete if quoted layers are missing. Show source, basis, uncertainty and unsupported engineering checks; unavailable evidence is not a passing result. Proposed drawing must make invalid width, unsupported curves, out-of-bound geometry and overlapping road footprints visible; intersection and overlap measurement rules require product review before a complete takeoff claim. Implemented route/width input validation, self-crossing and out-of-bound rejection, bounded simple footprint output, and separate road overlap rejection. One footprint counts its union once; junction allocation is unsupported. Preview failure retains the sketch and cannot confirm an obsolete result.

## Revision/history

Road geometry, layer recipe, factors, rates, source manifest and quantities are tied to the saved project/scenario revision. Relevant edits mark dependent schedules/estimates/readiness stale. Selected rate versions and issued proposals remain immutable; reprice/reissue is deliberate, and restore appends a revision rather than rewriting issued records. A future route, width or curve edit must follow the same expected-revision and stale-result rules as other geometry edits. Each confirmation applies one workspace undo step; explicit save persists with expected revision. Preview uses the existing authorised spatial-edit endpoint and checks current revision. Route edits preserve existing layer/rate metadata and regenerate quantities. Confirm/cancel the road sketch before switching options or saving.

## Explicit exclusions

No certified or automatic horizontal/vertical road design, engineered centerline-to-corridor offsets, curve-radius/superelevation, traffic/loading design, geotechnical/subgrade design, compaction prescription, survey/DEM accuracy, cut/fill/borrow/haul, drainage/hydrology, utilities, kerbs/markings/signage/traffic safety unless explicitly scoped later, permitting, jurisdiction compliance, engineering seal, guaranteed quote, supplier price feed or legal/field acceptance. Public/highway design is outside the bounded first slice. QGIS/GRASS/GDAL are data/profile/review aids, not engineering approval. The proposed route/width/curve preview would be an interactive concept tool, not an engineered alignment or approval.
