# Saved road and grass looks in 3D — 13 November 2026

The user reported that roads and painted ground disappeared or lost their looks after choosing 3D design scene. The map used procedural fill patterns/raster paint while Three.js used plain polygon colours and an opaque terrain mesh.

## Changes

- `layout-textures.ts` exports the existing road-image generator for reuse by both Mapbox and Three. Dirt, Pavement, Stone and Gravel use the same original pixel patterns. `scene-surfaces.ts` creates colour-correct repeating road textures, anchored in the metric frame with a 2 m visual tile (not a construction dimension).
- The saved geographic grass strokes use the same `renderGround` canvas, including all six looks, ordering, clipping and erasing. UVs use the existing sampled metric-to-Mercator frame, preserving north/south orientation and grid convergence. Both map-embedded and local metric design scenes receive the scenario's saved strokes. A render-only flat surface handles sites without a terrain model; it never creates survey/quantity data.
- `ObjectScene.tsx` retains textures through view/scenario switches and reload, with per-scene texture disposal. Paint follows the same ground topology, has transparent unpainted areas, and stays below roads/pond water. Selection tints retain the pattern. Only proposed roads with saved surface metadata receive that look; no construction assembly is inferred from a material name.
- Visual review caught road/ground intersections from independently triangulated surfaces. `draped-surface.ts` now clips road triangles against the rendered ground triangles, retaining barycentric ground references. Road heights update continuously during sculpting on those same planes. A cached spatial index bounds candidate searches. Geometry bounding boxes/spheres update with heights for picking. No display-derived road profile, earthworks quantity, rate or source is introduced.
