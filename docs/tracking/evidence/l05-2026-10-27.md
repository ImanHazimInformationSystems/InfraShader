## Implementation

`apps/api/app/plan_reference.py` validates a two-point similarity transform in the site's projected metric CRS. `workspace.py` owns scoped evidence checks, preview, revision persistence, restoration and trace review flags. `calculations.py` prevents issuing a snapshot while linked traces need review.

`apps/web/src/components/PlanReferencePanel.tsx` supports bounded PNG/JPEG and locally rasterised selected PDF pages, rights/source metadata, A/B pixel points, anchor, known distance, grid-north bearing, declared uncertainty, locking, visibility and opacity. `DrawingMap.tsx` renders the private raster and fits its bounds. `GeometryWorkspace.tsx` links applied target/work geometry to calibration, preserves geometry on replacement and requires explicit review. Domain schemas strip backend-derived fields from requests.

The original PDF remains on the device. The selected raster is private evidence. This is reference alignment, not CAD extraction, perspective correction or survey-grade conversion. Declared uncertainty is not a measured fit residual. See [user instructions](../../plan-reference.md).
