## What was verified and changed

- Existing `ExploreWorkspace`, `PlaceSearch`, `BoundsForm`, `SiteMap` and `ExploreSun` supply fullscreen/collapsible controls, search, WGS84 extent selection, provider-height 3D, sun/shadows and project transfer. `MapEnvironment`, `EnvironmentControls`, `weather-layer` and `use-solar-day` supply existing manual weather and time/day rendering. `apps/api/app/explore.py` validates stateless analytical requests. No backend/schema change was needed for this slice.
- `apps/web/src/services/map.ts` no longer permanently caches an unavailable style; callers receive independent style copies. Explicit retry refreshes the provider style.
- `SiteMap.tsx` exposes a map retry after provider/WebGL failure, retaining selected bounds and explorer date/time. Context loss is tracked separately from provider errors. Numeric selection remains usable without WebGL. Full map reconstruction resets view/weather controls; it does not promise to preserve every transient rendering setting.
- `services/explore-buildings.ts` centralises loaded-polygon selection. `ExploreSun.tsx` distinguishes loaded parts, supported provider heights, unknown heights, unsupported geometry/heights and capacity omissions. Counts describe loaded tile parts, not unique surveyed buildings or whole-area completeness. Backend omissions are incorporated. No height is invented for an unknown building.
- `ExploreWorkspace.tsx` and `styles.css` keep the floating environment readout away from the phone coordinate form while site controls are open.
- Existing backend authorisation and project validation remain in place. The new PostGIS transfer test checks a new extent/project and proves existing geometry and revisions are unchanged; provider buildings are not silently saved into the new workspace.
