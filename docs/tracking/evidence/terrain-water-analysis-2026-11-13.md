## Implemented

- `terrain_analysis.py`: positive/negative linear TIN integration clipped to the projected site, including sign crossings. Flat-start or supplied existing-grid reference; cut, fill, net fill, pond geometric storage, source/datum and methods. Round water uses 128 edges; overlapping ponds reject. In-situ geometric volumes only; no bulking/compaction/soil suitability/topsoil/disposal/productivity assumptions.
- `terrain.py`, `domain/terrain.ts`, `TerrainBuilder.tsx`: local CSV/text elevation matrix import with exact grid dimensions, finite complete values, metre/datum/frame/orientation/source confirmation. Values and source persist in private workspace JSON; raw file is not externally uploaded. Import explicitly replaces original/current heights and removes pond water, with undo. Original ground is retained through sculpting; erase/reset restores it; raise/dig offsets follow original nodes. No automatic survey verification, reprojection or DEM ingestion.
- `workspace.py`: automatic cut/fill m³ work records ignore submitted volume and use backend quantities. Existing sourced compatible rates, Decimal estimates, schedule/BOQ, source notes and terrain-change review are reused. No invented prices, machine hours or soil conversion. Manual resource quantities remain available.
- Surface-water screening: user-entered uniform rain, explicit constant infiltration over wet cells (including pond water), supplied roughness, four-neighbour cell-average Manning transfers with available-water and quarter-equilibrium limiters. Site edges are closed except up to eight listed capacity/inlet-elevation drains. Initial pond water is integrated. Retained outputs include rain, infiltration, initial/remaining storage, discharge, peak specified-drain discharge, mass residual, limiting count, time step and depth/direction frames. Steps 1–10 s, duration 1–120 min, at most 32×32 cells. Disconnected site fragments within a cell reject.
- `POST/GET .../terrain-studies`: project read/analyse grants, archive/write rules, saved revision checks, idempotent keys and immutable existing Artifact snapshots. Historical workspace/project/model results are stale. Ordinary artifact lists exclude the new kind to preserve existing schemas. No migration/dependency addition.
- `TerrainStudy.tsx`, `domain/terrain-study.ts`, `ObjectScene.tsx`, `terrain-mesh.ts`: retained study selector, input/source/method disclosure, summaries, blue depth cells, direction arrows and timeline/playback. Surfaces/arrows merge into two draw objects. Dirty/stale results and sculpting hide overlays; reduced motion stops playback. Film drapes over ground for visibility; its cell outline is not a flood boundary; arrows are not velocities. Map buildings/roads/trees are not automatic hydraulic barriers or roof catchments.

## Limits and research

This custom screening cell-storage model is not a validated shallow-water/pipe-network solver. Mass conservation does not establish grid/time convergence, field calibration or regulatory accuracy. Inputs require suitable ground, rain, infiltration, roughness and drain sources. Transfer limiting and cell averaging affect travel times; compare grid/time steps and independently qualified models before hydraulic design. Off-site inflow, pipes/backwater, momentum/velocity, groundwater, erosion, pumps, roof catchments, structural/geotechnical approval and certification remain excluded.
